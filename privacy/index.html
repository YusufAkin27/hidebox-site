Privacy Policy

HideBox, provided by Yusuf Akin. Effective date: 11 November 2026.

Short version: HideBox does not collect, receive or share any of your data. Everything you put in the vault stays encrypted on your device.

1. Summary

  • No account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK that collects data.
  • The Android release build does not request the INTERNET permission, and the app makes no network requests. The developer receives no user data at all.
  • Your items are encrypted on the device and are not backed up anywhere by us. If you forget your PIN or remove the app, the data cannot be recovered by anyone.

2. What stays on your device

Photos, videos, files, notes, scans, settings and any break-in photos (see below) are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. They are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is derived from your PIN using Argon2id and is bound to a hardware-backed key on the device. We have no access to any of it, and none of it is sent to us or to anyone else by the app.

There is no cloud sync and no backup feature. Only you can export a copy of your items, using the app’s export function, and what you do with an exported file is under your control.

3. Permissions and why the app asks for them

Each permission is used only for the purpose below, and only on your device.

  • Photos and media. To import the photos and videos you choose. If you ask for it, the app can delete the originals after import; it does this only when you request it.
  • Camera. To take photos and videos and to scan documents into the vault. Captured items are stored encrypted in the app, not in your photo library. The camera is also used for the optional break-in photo (next section).
  • Biometrics (Face ID, fingerprint). To unlock the app if you turn this on. Biometric checking is done by your operating system. The app only receives a yes or no result and never sees your biometric data.
  • Notifications. Optional local notice when an import has finished. The app does not use push notifications and does not contact a notification server.
  • Motion sensors. Optional shake-to-lock and face-down lock use the accelerometer only to lock the app. Readings are not stored or sent.
  • Microphone (iOS). If you record sound with the app’s features, iOS may ask for microphone access. Recorded sound is kept encrypted in the app on the device only. The Android release build does not include the microphone permission.
  • Saving to Photos (iOS). A file is added to your photo library only when you export it yourself.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in your device settings. Features that need it will then stop working, but your vault stays intact.

4. Break-in photo

Break-in photo is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, the app takes one front-camera photo after the number of wrong PIN attempts that you choose. The photo is stored encrypted on your device only, is shown only inside the app after a correct unlock, and is never transmitted. You can delete it at any time.

Such a photo may show another person. You are responsible for using this feature lawfully, including any notice or consent requirements that apply where you live.

5. Auto-erase and reset

If you enable auto-erase, the app permanently deletes the vault on the device after the number of wrong PIN attempts you choose. “Reset vault” does the same on request. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

6. Purchases

Optional subscriptions or a one-time purchase are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. The app receives only the purchase status from the store on your device so that it can unlock the paid features. We do not receive or store your payment details, name or store account details. Payments are governed by the store’s own terms and privacy policy.

7. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, share or transfer data, because we do not have any. The app contains no advertising or analytics code and no third-party SDK that collects data. Links to this policy or to terms open in your browser, which is a separate app with its own privacy practices.

8. Children

HideBox is not directed at children. Because the app collects nothing from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and the Turkish KVKK give you rights over personal data held by a controller, such as access, correction and deletion. Since the developer does not collect or process your personal data, there is nothing for us to provide, correct or delete. All your data is under your control on your device, and you can delete it at any time with Reset vault or by removing the app. If you contact us by email, we use your message and address only to reply and delete them when no longer needed.

10. Security and limits

We design HideBox to protect your items with strong, well-known cryptography, but no software can promise absolute security. Someone who knows your PIN, or who has your unlocked phone, can open the vault. A disguise icon or cover mode makes the app less obvious, but the app cannot be completely invisible on a device. There is no recovery: keep your PIN safe and export items you cannot afford to lose.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date. Material changes will also be reflected in the app or store listing where appropriate.
