HideBox

A private vault for the photos, videos, files and notes you would rather keep to yourself.

  • Encrypted on your device. Items are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is derived from your PIN and tied to a hardware-backed key on your phone.
  • No account, no server. HideBox does not connect to the internet, has no analytics and shows no ads. We never receive your data.
  • Import, capture, scan. Bring in items from your gallery or other apps, take photos and videos, and scan documents straight into the vault.
  • Quiet by design. Optional disguise icons and cover mode make the app less obvious to someone glancing at your phone. No app can be completely invisible on a device.

Frequently asked questions

I forgot my PIN. Can you recover my data?

No, and nobody can. Your vault key exists only on your device and is protected by your PIN. We have no copy, no account and no server to ask. The only way forward is to reset the vault from the lock screen, which permanently erases everything in it and lets you start again.

How do I move to a new phone?

Vault keys are bound to the phone they were created on, so the data cannot simply be restored on another device. Before you switch, open HideBox on the old phone and export the items you want to keep, then import them into HideBox on the new phone. Do this while you still have the old phone and your PIN.

How do purchases and restores work?

Optional Pro features are sold as a subscription or a one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play. The store handles payment and billing. Use the restore option in the app (on the Pro screen) to re-activate a purchase on a device signed in to the same store account. To cancel or manage a subscription, use your store account settings. For refunds, contact Apple or Google, as they process the payment.

How do I delete all my data?

Use Reset vault in the app: it permanently deletes everything in the vault on that device. Removing the app also removes its data. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is no other copy to delete. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

Is anything sent to you?

No. The app does not use the network and we do not collect any data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
